# Benchmark cards

One card per dataset or benchmark before it is used in any reported comparison.
Each card must state:

- **Provenance** — who produced it, when, from what.
- **Licence** — and whether it may be redistributed. (Usually: no.)
- **Point-in-time integrity** — can availability times be reconstructed, or is
  the archive a snapshot of the present pretending to be the past? This is the
  question that decides whether a dataset can support a cutoff-safe claim at all.
- **Known biases** — media attention, language coverage, geographic coverage,
  coding error rates.
- **What it cannot measure** — stated explicitly.

No cards yet: M0 runs on a synthetic world, which is described in
`src/pramaanx/ingest/connectors/synthetic.py` and is not a benchmark.
