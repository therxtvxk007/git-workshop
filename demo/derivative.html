    Three colors, and they mean roles — not names. The curve is the given, the secant is scaffolding, the tangent is the conclusion. Nothing is colored because of what it is; everything is colored by the job it does in the argument. That is why the secant and the tangent are different colors even though, in the limit, they are the same line.

    The limit itself has no color. h is the quantity being pushed to zero, and it is carried entirely by motion, position, and the readout — never by a fourth hue. A limit is a process, and process is shown through change over time.
