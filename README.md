# The 3D Trait Locator — OCD × ADHD × Schizophrenia

A dimensional, self-reflection framework that places a person as a **single point in a 3D space**
whose axes are **Obsessive–Compulsive (O)**, **Attention/Hyperactivity (A)**, and
**Schizophrenia-spectrum / Schizotypy (S)** traits.

> ⚠️ **Not a diagnostic tool.** This is educational self-reflection. No questionnaire diagnoses
> OCD, ADHD, or a schizophrenia-spectrum condition — only a qualified clinician can. High scores
> mean "traits worth discussing with a professional," nothing more. In distress? Contact a
> clinician or crisis line.

## Why 3D and not three separate tests

The last decade of psychiatry has moved from **categorical** diagnosis ("do you have X?") toward
**dimensional / transdiagnostic** models — everyone sits *somewhere* on each continuum:

- **RDoC** (NIMH) organizes psychopathology by transdiagnostic functional domains and neural
  circuits rather than DSM categories.
- **HiTOP** arranges traits on continua from sub-clinical to clinical, up to a general "p-factor"
  that spans compulsive, psychotic, and neurodevelopmental symptoms.
- **2025 genetics** — the *Nature* cross-disorder study mapped **428 variants shared across ≥2 of
  14 psychiatric disorders** and 101 shared genomic hot-spots; schizophrenia and OCD show a
  **positive genetic correlation (r_g ≈ 0.36)**, and ADHD/OCD share neurocognitive
  (inhibitory-control) traits.

So the three conditions are **correlated axes, not disjoint boxes** — which is exactly why a 3D
position (with genuine co-elevation) describes reality better than picking one label.

## The three axes and their instrument grounding

| Axis | Construct | Grounded in | Sub-domains sampled |
|---|---|---|---|
| **O** | Obsessive–compulsive | **OCI-R** | washing · checking · ordering · obsessing · hoarding · mental neutralizing (+ doubt, "just-right", inflated responsibility) |
| **A** | Attention / hyperactivity–impulsivity | **DSM-5 / ASRS-5** | 9 inattention items + restlessness, "driven by a motor", impulsivity |
| **S** | Schizophrenia-spectrum / schizotypy | **O-LIFE + CAPE-42** | positive (unusual experiences) · cognitive disorganization · negative (introvertive anhedonia) · disorganization |

## Scoring

Each item is rated **0–4** (not at all → extreme & interfering) for the last 6 months.
Axis raw = 0–48 → normalized to **0–100**. Bands per axis: **0–24 low · 25–49 sub-threshold ·
50–74 elevated · 75–100 high**. The tool flags when two axes are both ≥ 50 (expected co-occurrence)
and points you to the overlap map to distinguish them.

## Important limitations

- Self-report only; no collateral history, no clinical interview, no ruling-out of other causes
  (sleep, trauma, substances, medical conditions, autism, mood/anxiety disorders).
- The schizophrenia axis measures **sub-clinical schizotypy / psychosis-proneness** traits that
  most people have to some degree — it is **not** a screen for active psychosis.
- Cut-offs here are illustrative bands, not clinically validated thresholds.
- Culture, context, and neurodiversity shape what counts as "excessive." Difference is not disease.
